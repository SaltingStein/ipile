Campaign pages are landing pages for launches, events, sales and causes. Pick a design, replace the starter text, add the sections you need and publish. Everything is in **Admin → Campaigns → Campaign pages**.

## Create a page

1. Go to **Admin → Campaigns → Campaign pages**.
2. Under **New campaign page**, pick a template:
   - **Spotlight**: a full-width banner or video with a bold headline. Good for launches.
   - **Split**: the headline beside a picture, a video or your sign-up form. Good for registrations.
   - **Story**: a calm column for longer text. Good for causes and detailed offers.
3. Enter a **Page name** and choose **Create page**. It opens as a draft with starter text to replace.

You can switch to another template at any time under **Design**; your content stays.

## Edit the page

The builder has three tabs. The **Live preview** on the right shows your changes as you type; choose **Full screen** to see the page at full size.

- **Content**
  - **Top of the page**: the headline, a short text, up to two buttons, and a banner (a picture, a YouTube or Vimeo video, or none).
  - **Sections**: open a section to edit it. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle (or use ↑ and ↓) to change the order, ⧉ to copy it and ✕ to remove it.
  - **Add a section**: choose a type. It goes under the section that is open.
- **Design**: the template and the accent colour used for buttons and highlights.
- **Settings**: the page name, its web address, a description for search engines and social media, the end date, the home page option and the page's results.

Choose **Save** often. You are warned if you try to leave with unsaved changes.

## Section types

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Text | A heading and paragraphs |
| Features | Up to 6 benefits in a grid |
| Picture | An uploaded picture with a caption |
| Video | A YouTube or Vimeo video |
| Call to action | A heading with one or two buttons |
| Form | One of your site forms, so visitors can sign up on the page |
| Countdown | Days, hours, minutes and seconds to a date |
| Questions | Questions and answers that open when clicked |
| Quote | A testimonial with the person's name and role |
| Figures | Up to 4 big numbers, such as 10k+ attendees |

## Buttons

Each button has a label and a link:

- a full web address, such as `https://example.com/tickets`;
- a page on your site, such as `/blog`;
- `#signup` to jump to the page's form, or `#details` to jump to the first section;
- or an email (`mailto:`) or phone (`tel:`) link.

Choose **Filled** for the main action and **Outline** for a second one.

## Add a sign-up form

1. Build and publish the form first in **Admin → Forms**.
2. In the campaign page, add a **Form** section and choose the form.

Answers are saved with the form's other responses in Admin → Forms, and each one counts as a sign-up for the campaign. A page can have one form. In the **Split** template the form appears beside the headline.

## Publish and share

1. Choose **Publish**. The page goes live at `/c/` followed by its address, for example `/c/summer-sale`.
2. A window opens where you can copy the link or add it to the site menu.
3. To take the page down, choose **Unpublish**. Drafts are only visible to people who manage campaigns.

## End a campaign

Under **Settings → End date**, pick when the campaign ends and write the message visitors see afterwards. Once it ends, the buttons and the form are hidden and the message is shown instead.

## Use a campaign as the home page

Under **Settings → Home page**, tick **Show this page at the site's home page**. The campaign replaces the landing page until you untick it, unpublish the page or it ends; then the normal landing page comes back by itself.

## See how it is doing

The list of campaign pages shows each page's views, button clicks, sign-ups and sign-up rate. Each visitor's views are counted once per visit, and your own visits while managing campaigns are not counted.

## Copy or delete a page

- **Copy** (in the list) or **Copy as a new draft** (in Settings) makes a draft with the same design and content, ready for the next campaign.
- **Delete page** removes it; its address stops working. Sign-ups stay with the form. Pictures are deleted once no other page uses them.

Anyone with the **Marketing** role, or the `manage_campaigns` permission, can build campaign pages. An administrator can give it in **Admin → Roles & permissions**.
