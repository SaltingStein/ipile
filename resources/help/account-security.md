Everything here is under **Account security** in the menu. You may be asked to confirm your password before the page opens.

## Change your password

Enter your current password, then your new password twice, and choose **Save password**. If you signed up with a connected account (for example Google) and never set a password, you can create one here.

## Connected accounts

If you link an account such as Google, you can sign in with it instead of your password. Choose **Disconnect** to remove the link.

## Sign out other devices

Under **Browser sessions** you can see where your account is signed in. Choose **Log out other browser sessions** and confirm your password to sign out everywhere except the device you are using.

## Recent security activity

This list shows sign-ins, password and two-factor changes, and other sensitive actions on your account. **If something there was not you**, change your password straight away and sign out your other sessions, then tell an administrator.
