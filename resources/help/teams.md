Teams let several people work together. Open **Teams** from the menu.

## Create a team

Under **Create a team**, enter a name (and an optional description) and choose **Create team**. You become the team's **owner**.

## Invite people

Open the team and use **Invite people**. Each person gets an email with a link to join. **The link works for 7 days.** Pending invitations are listed on the team page, where you can cancel them.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including deleting the team and handing it to someone else. |
| Admin | Rename the team, invite and remove members, and change roles. |
| Member | See the team and its members. |

## Switch team

If you belong to several teams, pick the one you are working in from the list at the top of the page, or open the team and choose to make it your current team.

## Leave a team

Open the team and choose **Leave** next to your name. The owner cannot leave without first handing the team to someone else.

## Owner actions

The owner can **transfer ownership** to another member, or **delete the team**. Deleting removes the team for everyone and cannot be undone.
