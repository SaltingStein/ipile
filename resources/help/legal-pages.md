Your site comes with draft Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions and Cookie Policy pages. Visitors only see a page after it is published, and they always see the last published version while you work on the next one.

## Set your names and contact address first

The drafts mention your app, your business and a contact address. Instead of typing these into each page, set them once:

1. Open **Admin → Branding**.
2. Check the **App name**.
3. Enter your **Organisation name**, for example the registered name of your company. If you leave it blank, the app name is used.
4. Enter a **Service name** if your legal pages should name the service differently from the app. If you leave it blank, the app name is used.
5. Enter a **Contact email** people can write to about privacy and legal questions.
6. Choose **Save branding**.

Your copyright line in the site footer also uses the organisation name.

## Use placeholders in your pages

Write these in any legal page, including its title, and they are replaced when the page is shown:

| Write | Shows |
|---|---|
| [service name] | Your service name, or your app name if none is set |
| [organisation name] | Your organisation name |
| [contact email] or [privacy contact] | Your contact email |

Capital letters do not matter. If you change a value in **Branding** later, every published page shows the new value straight away; you do not need to publish again. A placeholder with no value set stays on the page as written, so you can spot it.

## Edit and publish a page

1. Open **Admin → Legal pages** and choose the page.
2. Change the **Title** and **Content**. The **Draft preview** shows the page with your placeholders filled in.
3. Choose **Save draft**.
4. When the draft is ready, choose **Publish saved draft**. People with permission to publish see this button.

If someone else saved the page while you were editing, you will be asked to reload before saving or publishing.
