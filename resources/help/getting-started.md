## The dashboard

After you sign in you land on the **Dashboard**, your home page. You can always get back to it from the menu.

## The menu

The menu on the left lists everything you can open. On a phone, tap **Menu** at the top of the page to show it.

You only see the pages you are allowed to use. If you expect a page that is not there, ask an administrator to check your role.

## Teams

If you belong to more than one team, choose the team you are working in from the list at the top of the page. What you see can change with the team you pick. See [Teams](/help/teams) for more.

## Guest accounts

If you started without creating an account, you are using a **guest account**. Choose **Create an account** in the banner at the top of the page to keep your activity and sign in from other devices.

## Getting help

Come back to **Help** in the menu at any time. Use the search box to find an article quickly.
