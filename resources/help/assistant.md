The assistant answers questions in plain language. Your site may offer the built-in assistant or a third-party chat widget. Both are available only to people with a full account, when your administrator has enabled them.

## Open the assistant

For the built-in assistant, open **Assistant** in the menu on the left. The sections below describe its features and protections.

If your site uses a third-party widget, open its chat bubble on a workspace page. Its service decides what it can answer and which data you may see. The built-in assistant's protections and message limits described below do not apply to this widget. If it is unavailable, contact an administrator.

## What you can ask

- How to do things on the site, for example "How do I invite someone to my team?". It answers from the Help Center and links the article.
- About your own account, for example whether two-factor sign-in is on.
- About your own teams, for example who is in them and their roles.
- Business figures, if your administrator allows them and your role covers them, for example "How many sign-ups did the summer campaign get?".

Type your question and press **Enter** or choose **Send**. Choose **New conversation** to start again. Each person can send a limited number of messages a day.

## Staff modes

If you help run the site and your role allows it, the assistant can also answer in a staff mode. You do not need to choose one: each question goes to the right mode automatically, and the answer is labelled with it.

- **Site guidance:** questions about running the site, for example "Where do I set up a payment integration?" or "How does threat monitoring decide what is high risk?". It answers from the site's internal guides as well as the Help Center.
- **Analytics:** questions about the figures you may see, for example "How many sign-ups did we get this month compared with last month?".

The page shows which staff modes you have, under the warning. A short follow-up such as "and last month?" stays in the same mode; **New conversation** starts afresh. The same protections apply in every mode.

## What the assistant never does

The assistant never gives out, guesses or helps you obtain:

- passwords (including your own), sign-in codes, one-time codes, recovery codes or two-factor secrets;
- API keys, payment keys or other secrets;
- other people's details, such as their emails, phone numbers, payments or form answers;
- protected system data.

**Asking for any of these signs you out at once on every device, and the administrators are told.** Depending on your site's settings, your account is then either suspended until an administrator restores it, or you can sign back in with your password and a code from your authenticator app. Accounts without two-factor sign-in are always suspended until an administrator restores them.

To change or reset your password, or to set up two-factor sign-in, ask the assistant *how* to do it, or see [Account security](/help/account-security).
