Forms let your team collect information from anyone, for example registrations for a conference, workshop sign-ups or feedback after an event. You build the form by dragging in the fields you need, so nothing has to be set up for you in advance.

Forms belong to a team. Choose the team at the top of the page before you start. The team owner and team admins can create and edit forms and read the responses. Other members can see the list of forms and their links.

## Create a form

1. Choose **Forms** in the menu.
2. Under **Create a form**, pick a starting point: **Blank form**, **Event registration**, **Contact us** or **Feedback survey**.
3. Choose **Create form**. The form builder opens.

New forms start as a **Draft**, which only your team's owner and admins can see.

## Copy a form for reuse

1. On **Forms**, find a form you can manage and choose **Copy as draft**.
2. The builder opens a new form named **Copy of …**. Rename it and review its questions and settings.
3. Set a new closing time if needed, then publish when you are ready.

The copy stays in the same team and gets a new web address. It keeps the saved questions, wording, page layout, banner, response limit and confirmation-email setting. Its closing time is cleared, and it starts as an unpublished **Draft** with no responses. Copying does not change the original form or its responses.

Each form has its own banner copy, so replacing or deleting one does not remove the other's banner. If copying fails, no partial form is kept; check the original banner and try again.

## Add and arrange fields


The builder has three parts: **Add a field** on the left, your form in the middle and **Settings** on the right.

- **Add a field:** drag a field type onto the form and drop it where you want it, or click the field type to add it below the selected field.
- **Reorder:** drag a field up or down, or use the **↑** and **↓** buttons on the field.
- **Copy or remove:** use **⧉** to duplicate a field and **✕** to remove it.
- **Change a field:** click it, then edit it under **Field** in the settings. You can change the question, add help text, mark it **Required** and, for choice fields, type the **Options, one per line**.

Field types include short and long answers, email, phone number, number, date, website link, dropdown, single choice, multiple choice, a **Yes / I agree** tick box and **Section heading** to split a long form into parts.

Choose **Save** when you are done. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, you will be asked first.

## Publish and share

1. Open the **Form** tab in the settings.
2. Set the **Web address** people will use, for example `lagos-tech-summit`.
3. Choose **Published** and then **Save**.
4. The first time you publish, **Your form is live** opens. Choose **Copy link** and share it by email, chat or social media. Anyone with the link can fill in the form, with or without an account.

You can open the same window later with **Share** at the top of the builder, or use **Copy link** on the **Forms** page.

**Add to a menu:** if you manage the site's menus, **Your form is live** also lets you add the form to the **Public site menu** (the header of your public pages) or the **Workspace sidebar**. Choose **Add** next to the menu you want. Otherwise, send the link to your site's administrators and ask them to add it.

## Design the form page

Open the **Design** tab in the builder:

1. Under **Page layout**, choose **Simple** (a clean, centred form) or **Designed** (a large banner behind the title, with the closing date and places left beside the form). Choose **Save** to apply it.
2. Under **Banner image or video**, choose a file. It is saved as soon as it uploads, and replaces any banner you had before.
   - Images: JPG, PNG or WebP. Wide images, about 1600 × 600, look best.
   - Videos: MP4 or WebM. In the **Designed** layout, videos play silently on a loop behind the title.
   - The largest size allowed is shown under the upload button. If a file is too big, make it smaller and try again.
3. To take the banner off, choose **Remove banner**.

## Limit places and set a closing time

Under the **Form** tab:

- **Response limit** stops the form when it reaches that many responses, for example the number of seats. Deleting a response frees a place.
- **Closes at** stops new responses at that date and time.
- **Closed** keeps the page up but stops new responses straight away.

People who open a full or closed form see a message instead of the questions.

## Send a confirmation email

Tick **Email a confirmation** under the **Form** tab. The form needs an **Email** field: the confirmation goes to the address entered there and includes your **Thank-you message**.

## See and download responses

1. On **Forms**, choose **Responses** next to the form.
2. Each response is a row, newest first. If someone was signed in, their account email is shown too.
3. Choose **Download CSV** to open every response in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

If you remove a field after people have answered it, their answers are kept and shown in a column marked **(removed)**.

## Delete a form

Open the form, go to the **Form** tab and choose **Delete form**. This also deletes all of its responses and cannot be undone. To keep the responses, download them first.
