The blog lets you publish news, event recaps and announcements on your site. Everything is in **Admin → Post manager → Blog**.

## Switch the blog on or off

1. Go to **Admin → Blog**.
2. Under **Blog settings**, tick **Blog is on**.
3. Give it a **Blog name** (for example Blog, News or Stories) and, if you like, an **Introduction**.
4. Choose **Save settings**.

While the blog is on, visitors can read your published posts and the blog appears in the site menu. While it is off, visitors cannot open it, but you can still write and preview posts, ready for launch.

## Write a post

1. In **Admin → Blog**, choose **New post**.
2. Enter a **Title**. The **Web address** fills in from the title; you can change it before you first save.
3. Add a **Summary** if you want to control the text shown on the blog page and in social media previews.
4. Write the post under **Content**, much like in a word processor. Use **Preview** to see exactly how it will look on the blog.
5. Choose **Save draft**. Drafts are only visible to people who manage the blog.

## Format your text

Use the toolbar above the text:

- **Normal text / Heading / Subheading** to change the style of a paragraph.
- **B**, *I* and ~~S~~ for bold, italic and strikethrough. Shortcuts work too: Ctrl+B for bold and Ctrl+I for italic (⌘ on a Mac).
- **🔗** to add a link: select the words, choose 🔗, type the web address and choose **Apply**. Choose 🔗 again on a link to change it or **Remove link**.
- **• List** and **1. List** for bulleted and numbered lists, **❝** for a quote, **―** for a divider line.
- **↶** and **↷** to undo and redo.

## Add pictures to a post

- Choose **🖼 Image** and pick one or more pictures, or paste or drag a picture straight into the text. JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF are accepted; the size limit is shown under the editor.
- Click a picture to select it, then fill in **Image description** to say what it shows. This helps people who use screen readers and also helps search engines.
- To remove a picture, click it and press Delete. Pictures you take out of a post are deleted from the site when you save, unless another post uses them. Pictures you uploaded but never saved in a post are cleared away after a day.

## Add a YouTube or Vimeo video

1. Choose **▶ Video** in the toolbar.
2. Paste the video's link (from the address bar or the video's **Share** button) and choose **Insert**.

The video plays right inside the post. If you prefer, you can also paste a YouTube or Vimeo link on a line of its own; it becomes a player when the post is shown. A link in the middle of a sentence stays a normal link.

## Sort posts into categories

Categories group posts by topic, such as Events or Announcements. Visitors see them as buttons above the posts and can open each category's page.

1. In **Admin → Blog**, go to **Categories**, type a name under **New category** (and a description if you like) and choose **Add category**.
2. When writing a post, tick its categories under **Categories** on the right. A post can be in several. You can also add a new category there without leaving the post.
3. Choose **Rename** to change a category's name; its web address stays the same, so shared links keep working. **Remove** takes the category away but keeps its posts.

If you prefer writing in Markdown, the **Markdown** tab shows the post as plain text you can edit directly.

## Add a cover image

Under **Cover image**, choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file (about 1600 × 900 looks best), then save the post. To take it off, tick **Remove the cover image** and save.

## Publish or schedule

1. Under **Publishing**, choose **Published**.
2. Leave **Publish at** empty to publish straight away, or pick a future date and time to schedule the post.
3. Choose **Save and publish**.

Scheduled posts appear on the blog automatically at their time. To unpublish a post, choose **Draft** and save.

## Preview, edit and delete

- **Preview** next to a post in **Admin → Blog** shows it as visitors will see it, even if it is a draft or the blog is off. A banner reminds you it is a preview.
- To delete a post, open it and choose **Delete post**. Its cover and the pictures only it used are deleted too. This cannot be undone.

## RSS feed

Feed readers and newsletter tools can follow the blog at `/blog/feed`.
