Visitors can read your public pages in other languages: the landing page, blog, forms, campaign pages and legal pages. Signed-in pages stay in English. Translation is off until you switch it on in **Admin → Appearance → Languages**.

## Switch translation on

1. Go to **Admin → Appearance → Languages**.
2. Tick **Translation is on**.
3. Under **Languages to offer**, tick the languages you want, for example French, Yoruba or Hausa.
4. Under **Automatic translation**, choose an AI model to translate for you, or **Off** to write every translation yourself. To use an AI model, first switch one on in **Admin → Integrations → AI models**.
5. Set **Most texts translated per day** to keep the cost in check.
6. Choose **Save languages**.

A language menu (🌐) now appears at the top of public pages. Each visitor's choice is remembered on their device.

## How texts get translated

You do not need to list your texts. When visitors open a page in another language, the site notes the text that has no translation yet. The AI model then translates it, every 10 minutes, up to your daily limit. Choose **Translate now** under **Progress** to translate straight away.

To prevent misuse, a text is only translated once a second visitor has seen it too. Text one person made up is never translated and is cleared away after a week. Text you see yourself while managing languages or campaigns counts straight away. To prepare a language before launch, open your public pages in that language.

Only page text is sent to the AI model, never anyone's personal details.

## Correct a translation

1. Under **Translations**, choose the language. Use **Show** to filter, for example **Not translated**, or **Search**.
2. Change the text in the box next to the original and choose **Save**.

Your wording is marked as written by a person and is never replaced by the AI model. To let the AI model try again, empty the box and save. **Remove text** deletes a text you no longer use; it comes back if a page still shows it.

## Good to know

- Your brand name and anything marked as not to be translated, such as code and dates, stay as they are.
- Arabic pages read right to left.
- Switching translation off removes the language menu but keeps your translations for later.
