Some actions matter more than others, for example moving money, changing bank details or changing who can manage your account. Before these, you may be asked for a fresh code from your authenticator app, even though you are already signed in. This means that someone who gets hold of your open session, for example on a shared computer, still cannot do them.

## Confirm it's you

1. On the **Confirm it's you** page, open your authenticator app.
2. Enter the six-digit code under **Authentication code**.
3. Choose **Confirm**.

You go straight back to what you were doing. For the next few minutes you are not asked again in the same browser. A different browser or device always asks.

## No access to your authenticator app?

Choose **Use a recovery code** and enter one of the recovery codes you saved when you turned on two-factor authentication. Each recovery code works only once.

## Not using an authenticator app yet?

You are taken to **Account security** to set one up first (see [Two-factor authentication](/help/two-factor-authentication)). When setup is finished, go back to what you were doing and carry on.

## If your code is not accepted

- Check that the time on your phone is set automatically. Codes depend on the clock.
- Wait for a new code and try again. A code that has already been used does not work twice.
- After several wrong codes you have to wait a minute before trying again.

If you keep being asked but did not start anything important, change your password and sign out of other sessions from **Account security**.
