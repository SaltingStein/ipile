In **Admin → Forms** you build forms that belong to the whole site rather than one team, using the same drag-and-drop builder teams use (see [Build forms and collect responses](/help/forms)).

## Add questions to the sign-up page

The sign-up page always asks for name, email and password. To collect more, for example a company, job title or how people heard about you:

1. Go to **Admin → Forms** and open **Sign-up questions**.
2. Drag in the fields you need. The built-in fields are shown with a lock at the top and cannot be changed.
3. Open the **Form** tab, choose **Published** and then **Save**.

Your questions now appear on the sign-up page below the password. Required questions must be answered before an account is created. Answers appear under **Responses**, next to each new account's email.

To stop asking, choose **Draft** and **Save**. Answers already given are kept. The sign-up questions cannot be deleted.

To ask these questions during connected-account sign-up, open **Admin → Security → Sign-in & 2FA**, select **Require profile completion for connected sign-up** and choose **Save sign-in rules**. New connected-account users then answer the published questions before their account is created. Existing linked accounts do not repeat them.

## Create a site form

Under **Create a site form**, pick a template, choose **Create form**, then build and publish it as you would a team form. Use site forms for things like a public contact form or a waitlist.

## Copy a site form

Choose **Copy as draft** next to a form in **Admin → Forms**. The copy remains a site form and opens in the builder with a new web address and no responses. Review it before publishing. See [Copy a form for reuse](/help/forms) for the settings that carry over.

Copying **Sign-up questions** creates an ordinary site form from those extra questions. It does not change the sign-up page or include its built-in name, email and password fields.
