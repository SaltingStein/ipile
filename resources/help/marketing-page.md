The community marketing page introduces Ìpìlẹ̀ as a free boilerplate from SaltinGStein Ltd. Visitors can explore its features and open the public GitHub repository without an account.

The **Markov intelligence. AI clarity.** section explains how Aegis combines unusual-sequence detection, account risk tracking and optional AI incident explanations. AI suggests next steps for administrator review; your security settings control automated responses.

## Navigate on mobile

On a phone or smaller screen, choose **Menu** in the page header to open the navigation. Choose a section to jump to it; the menu closes after your selection. Choose **Close menu** to hide it without leaving the page.

## Show the marketing page

1. Open **Admin → Campaigns → Marketing page**.
2. Select **Enable marketing page**.
3. Check the **GitHub repository URL**. Every **Get Ìpìlẹ̀ — it’s free** button opens this repository.
4. Choose **Save marketing settings**.
5. Choose **View home page** or **View marketing page** to check the result.

While enabled, the marketing page appears at the home address and at `/marketing`. It takes priority over a campaign homepage. Your campaign and theme selections are kept.

## Hide the marketing page

Clear **Enable marketing page** and choose **Save marketing settings**. The current campaign or theme homepage returns immediately. The `/marketing` address becomes unavailable.

The marketing page starts switched off. Only people with permission to manage site settings can change it.
